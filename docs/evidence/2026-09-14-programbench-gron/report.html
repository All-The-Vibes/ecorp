Methodology

  • Reads existing official evaluator output; this generator launches no benchmark work.
  • The official active-branch and ignored-test filters apply before scoring.
  • Executed = eligible passed or failure cases matched to recorded run_tests and results_read JUnit evidence.
  • Errors can occur in setup; errors, skipped, system_error and not_run are reported separately from executed cases.
  • A complete program score uses the official retained-test denominator, including skipped, error and not_run outcomes.
  • The pilot mean is available only when every selected real program has complete, consistent candidate and evaluator evidence.
  • Missing candidate/output is not scored as a benchmark failure. Unknown programs and fixtures receive no score.
  • No full-benchmark leaderboard score is claimed. Coverage and pilot mean are distinct.
  • Local log consistency does not independently authenticate inference, ECorp lineage, source independence, or network isolation.
