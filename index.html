Why operations is still below 90

Capabilities need operating evidence

Three outcomes remain under-evidenced

  1. 1Governed recurring delivery. Repeatable tools and a short local cadence do not yet establish sustained, coordinated maintenance delivery.
  2. 2Learning that improves later behavior. Candidates, advisory references and repair tests do not prove authenticated rule promotion or better later decisions.
  3. 3Accepted agent contributions. The API evidence window classified none of 91 merged PRs as agent-authored; four of 277 commits carried agent co-authorship.

Read the limits precisely

Local restoration was verified. Yet all judges kept self-healing at 3/4, citing the pack’s static hasRollbackPath:false signal alongside its observed automaticRollbackVerified:true. This is an evidence-model tension, not proof that native restoration is absent.

Raising automation and improvement loops alone to 4/4 would yield 81.25 operations. If D3 remains 3 and D9 remains 2, every other dimension must reach 4 to yield 90.5.

These are arithmetic bounds, not predicted scores. No scoring rules or authorship history were changed.
